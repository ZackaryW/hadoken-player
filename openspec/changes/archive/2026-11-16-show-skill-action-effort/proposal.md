## Why

Skill details describe actions without showing their effort, so players cannot see the priority that already governs conflicts.

## What Changes

- Show a read-only effort number beside each action or restriction mentioned in skill details.
- Show manual activation effort for manual-capable skills.
- Keep concise descriptions and derive numbers from code-defined values.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
None.

### Modified Capabilities
- `player-skills`: visible action, block and activation effort in on-demand details.

## Impact

Independent skill metadata, the skill details dialog, focused widget coverage and the player-skills specification. No storage or reconciliation changes.
